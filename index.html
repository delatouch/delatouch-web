About

A. Q., aka Delatouch, is a multidisciplinary artist and creative director. With more than 30 years of experience, he has worked for numerous international brands and industries across every sector requiring creative and design solutions.

Coming from advertising, brand creation, illustration and urban art, and heavily influenced by visual avant-garde, sci-fi, comics, fashion and music, he has worked in Spain (his home country), China, Germany, and is now based in Switzerland.

His work has spanned cartoon drawing, advertising graphics, sign painting, full brand creation and concept, rebranding, packaging design and production, fashion, footwear and accessories design and production, expert photo retouching, web design, app design, typeface creation, character design, VJing, muralism, and even window display design.

Now building DTH_Graphix.
